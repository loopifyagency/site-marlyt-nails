# Surface brief: index.html (home, single-page site)

## Scope and visitor mode
Persuade. One-page marketing site for Marlyt_nails, onglerie in Brest. The visitor decides and acts: call 07 84 55 91 65 to book.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- Audience: women in and around Brest, arriving from Google Maps on a phone; regulars and newcomers who want precision, patience and warmth.
- Job: see real work, trust the person, find hours and address, call.
- Action: `tel:+33784559165` ("Prendre rendez-vous"), repeated in hero, nav, services, CTA, footer, floating mobile button.
- Proof on hand: five real photos (about 200-224 px, upscaled 3x; display no larger than about 340 px wide on desktop), five verbatim reviews, real hours and address. No prices, years, client counts, social handles or e-mail exist: none may be invented.
- Brief requires: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, loader, parallax hero, marquee, a pinned storytelling section, a scroll-driven horizontal section, counters, immersive gallery, review carousel, strong CTA, Google Maps iframe linking to https://maps.app.goo.gl/iuJpvy9DYGNE1eFx5, giant footer name, LocalBusiness JSON-LD, reduced-motion, mobile floating call button.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The lacquer shade rack of a nail studio, scaled up to the whole site: choosing a shade repaints the page. It refuses the pink-and-white salon template (thin serif, rounded cards, soft gradient hero beside a hand photo).
OWN-WORLD: Press-on nail-tip swatches in three real shapes (ovale, carré, amande) with one lacquer gloss streak each; numbered shade tags; a fan-deck pivot; Blanc neige ground with fields of Cerise, Fuchsia, Griotte, Dragée and Givre; section seams are lacquer drips; Caprasimo display with Hanken Grotesk, tabular numerals on tags.
STORY: A visitor from Google Maps sees careful work on real hands within seconds, reads five real clients describing Maryna's precision and kindness, trusts it, and calls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Blanc neige ground; the wordmark "Marlyt_nails" across the full width in giant Caprasimo; in front of it a fan of seven nail swatches standing from the bottom edge on a pivot (three solid shades, four real photos, each tagged with its nuancier number); one-line promise and "Prendre rendez-vous" (tel:) lower left; tapping a swatch spreads that shade across the whole hero.
FORM: Le Nuancier, the user's pick over the dealt "Cahier de rendez-vous"; position 1 on my ordered list; seed:77ff41488dddbbad.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Memorable moment
Loader: a nail is painted cerise by a single brush stroke, grows to fill the screen, and lifts away on a lacquer drip edge into the shade rack. Signature interaction: shade tap repaints the hero; pinned story paints one big nail from bare to glossy as you scroll.

## Unresolved decisions
- Service menu is authored from what photos and reviews prove, no prices; client must validate.
- Key figures use only supplied facts (5 days, 31 h, 10 nails).
- No social links or e-mail supplied; footer omits them.
- Higher-resolution photos requested from client.
- Shade numbers (N°01 to N°07) are one system: hero tags, service cards and gallery numerals all cite them.
