# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no framework and no build step. GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis are named by the brief and self-hosted under `vendor/`. Deploy target not stated; static files fit any host.

## Users
Women in and around Brest looking for a careful, reliable nail technician: regulars who have gone back to the same hands for years, and newcomers tired of "salons after salons" who want precision, patience and listening. They arrive on a phone, often from Google Maps, and want to see the work, trust the person, and call.

## Product Purpose
Marlyt_nails is a one-person manicure studio in Brest run by Maryna (named in all five client reviews). The site exists to turn a visit into a phone call: show the work, convey the warmth of the space, state prices honestly once supplied, give hours and address. Success is a booked appointment (call to 07 84 55 91 65).

## Positioning
Haute-precision manicure at fair prices in a cocooning, personal setting: every review pairs "perfectionniste / minutieuse / propre" with "gentillesse / douceur / à l'écoute", and notes the price is low relative to the quality and that she accommodates rescheduling. A chain or a nail bar cannot truthfully claim this one person.

## Operating Context
Studio at 11 Rue Maurice Denis, 29200 Brest. Hours (may change): Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 09:00-12:00 and 13:30-17:00; Wed 09:00-12:00 and 15:00-17:00; Sat closed; Sun closed (Toussaint noted as closed). Appointments are by phone. Google Maps short link: https://maps.app.goo.gl/iuJpvy9DYGNE1eFx5

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Primary action: book an appointment by calling 0784559165 (`tel:`). No online booking tool, email or social handle was supplied; none may be invented.
- Services list and prices were "to be completed". Confirmed approach: write a service menu from what the photos and reviews prove, with NO prices; the client validates it.
- Key figures: only verifiable ones, derived from the supplied data (5 days open per week, 31 opening hours per week computed from the hours above, 10 nails per appointment). No invented years of experience or client counts.
- Language: French. Contrast, alt text, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support required.
- Only five photos exist (about 200-224 px wide); they cannot carry full-bleed layouts.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Marlyt_nails (Onglerie à Brest). Tone: feminine and warm.
- Imposed colours: cerise, fuchsia, blanc neige.
- Reviews are reproduced verbatim from five named clients; never edited, never invented.

## Evidence on Hand
- Five client reviews (Justine Robilliart, Olga Dmi, Lucie Portello, Kathleen Dimanche, Clémentine Houssais), verbatim in the brief.
- Five photos of real work: red semi-permanent pedicure with small nail art; deep red glossy short oval gel; pink-to-white ombré (baby boomer) on short square nails; silver-white cat-eye/chrome almond nails; mirror dark-red chrome short nails.
- Absent, do not fabricate: prices, years in business, client counts, Instagram or other social links, e-mail, rating averages, certifications, studio photos, portrait of Maryna.

## Product Principles
1. Show the hands first: the work is the proof, and every claim traces to a photo, a review or a supplied fact.
2. Warmth and exactness together: gentle in voice, exacting in detail.
3. One action: every section ends in an easy path to calling.
4. Never fabricate: prices, numbers, handles and reviews are supplied or absent.
5. Honest about limits: low-resolution photos are framed and sized to stay crisp, not blown up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG AA contrast; full keyboard navigation; descriptive alt text; `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a calm version with no scroll-jacking; content visible by default if JavaScript fails.
